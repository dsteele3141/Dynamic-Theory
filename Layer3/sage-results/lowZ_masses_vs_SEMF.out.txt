[3] Verdict
    - The SEMF RMS for A<=10 is 4.6 MeV, so 'beating the SEMF' here is a
      LOW bar: the liquid-drop model is calibrated for heavy nuclei and
      is not a serious light-nucleus model.
    - Williams' 2.9 MeV is an IN-SAMPLE fit (level energies taken from
      experiment, p.276), not a parameter-free prediction; and his
      Eqs (9.88)-(9.93) are not reproduced verbatim here.
    - A fair test compares against a genuine light-nucleus model
      (ab initio / no-core shell model), NOT the SEMF.  The DT claim is
      PLAUSIBLE but, on the available text, NOT a demonstrated improvement.
