    required |Gdot/G| ~ 0.81 H0 ; direct bound ~ 1e-03 H0 -> exceeds by 806x

    Williams (1-H0 tau)^4 (Eq 3.55): |Delta m(0.5)| = 1.15 mag vs signal 0.40
  [PASS] Williams' power-4 law overshoots the signal by >2x (Eq 3.54->3.55 too steep).

  NET: the mechanism is SOUND and right-order -- an epoch-dependent
  Chandrasekhar candle (L ~ G^{-3/2}, G weakening at ~H0) reproduces the
  dark-energy magnitude residual in sign, size, AND redshift shape with one
  order-unity rate -> dark energy CAN be an evolving-candle artifact in DT.
  BUT: it trades Omega_Lambda for a fitted G-rate; that rate (~H0) is ~10^3x
  above direct |Gdot/G| bounds (the key objection); and Williams' specific
  (1-H0 tau)^4 law overshoots. Verdict: plausible in-framework, but in
  tension with independent G-variation limits -- NOT as clean as the
  rotation-curve/MOND result. Full write-up: mass-sector-postdictions.txt.
