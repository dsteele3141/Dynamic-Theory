[5] Confrontation with measurement
    Measured radiation pressure is POSITIVE across the spectrum:
      - microwave/radio pressure underwrites laser-power standards and

    tension.
  [PASS] DT's long-wavelength pressure sign CONTRADICTS the measured (+) sign.
