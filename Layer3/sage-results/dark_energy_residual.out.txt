  NET (answers):
  Q1 (how close): DT tracks the real Pantheon Hubble diagram to RMS=0.057 mag
     vs LambdaCDM's 0.036 mag -- an EXTRA ~45 millimag of scatter. EdS (no dark
     energy) is 0.185 mag off. So DT is a few-millimag near miss, not a failure.
  Q2 (what absorbs the residual -- no need for ONE effect):
     - a slightly richer candle (the next term in M_Ch(z), whose shape DT
       only fixes approximately) drops chi2 from 80 to 45 ~ LambdaCDM's 51;
     - the 1-param leftover scatter (~45 millimag) is AT the host mass-step
       systematic floor (~60 mmag); standard analyses absorb this via nuisance terms.
  So the residual has plausible homes (candle-shape + ordinary systematics);
  it does NOT by itself vindicate dark energy. The real discriminator stays the
  |Gdot/G| fork (script 26), not the Hubble-diagram residual.
