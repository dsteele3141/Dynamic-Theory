------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The moon systems do NOT rescue the quantised-orbit picture to a clean win:
  * each giant's regular moons CAN be laid on an r~n^2 ladder, but only at
    the same modest RMS (~0.14-0.21) as the planets -- numerology band, not <0.05;
  * the derived quantisation velocity w is NOT universal: at the min-RMS anchor
    Sun~239, Jupiter~139, Saturn~100, Uranus~33 km/s (spread ~7x); even taking
    the Sun at its Mercury=3 value (144) vs Uranus (33) is >4x. A single
    hbar_g does not fit all systems -- each carries its OWN fitted constant;
  * w is not even pinned WITHIN the Sun: the ground state n0 is degenerate
    (n0=3 -> 144 km/s, min-RMS n0=5 -> 239 km/s), so the 'constant' floats;
  * the sharpest sub-system (the Galileans) has its spacing FIXED by the 4:2:1
    Laplace mean-motion resonance -- a standard dynamical mechanism, so it
    cannot be counted as independent evidence for quantisation.
NET: adding the moon data (more constraint, not more parameters) leaves the
verdict INCONCLUSIVE and, if anything, strengthens the under-constrained
reading. A universal gravitational action is not supported by the satellites.
(Honest note: Jupiter ~139 sitting near the Sun's Mercury=3 value ~144 is the
one mild coincidence -- but Saturn/Uranus break it, and the n0 freedom lets w
drift, so it is not evidence for a shared constant.)
