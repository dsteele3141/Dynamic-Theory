VERDICT / READING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
From Jupiter outward the unfilled-orbit pattern is a SUPERPOSITION:
 * LADDER effect: adjacent giants sit ~4-6 levels apart (n ~ 11,15,21,26),
   because their real orbit-radius RATIOS (~1.6-2) map to delta-n ~ sqrt(ratio)
   at those n -- so ~3-5 EMPTY levels sit between each giant. Roughly constant,
   set by the geometry of an n^2 ladder over a ~geometric spacing.
 * FEEDING effect: those same giants are spaced only ~8-16 MUTUAL HILL RADII
   apart, and the Jupiter-Saturn pair (most massive) is the TIGHTEST in Hill
   radii -- a massive planet clears a wide zone, so the levels inside it stay
   empty. The +-3 R_H clearing width of each giant is ~1-3 levels, comparable
   to the observed adjacent empties -- the two pictures are CONSISTENT in size.
KEY CONTRAST: the terrestrials are MANY Hill radii apart yet occupy CONSECUTIVE
levels (no gaps), while the giants are FEW Hill radii apart yet leave 3-5 empty
levels each. So the empties are NOT set by the ladder alone nor by mass alone --
they are where a big planet's clearing zone happens to straddle several closely-
spaced high-n levels. Occupation = feeding/dynamics; allowed radii = ladder.
Neither effect needs the other, and standard feeding reproduces the pattern
without the ladder -- consistent with the 'inconclusive' verdict.
